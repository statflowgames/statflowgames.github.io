Arsenia Chronicle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Developer: statflow (StatFlow Co.)

statflow (StatFlow Co., "we") respects the privacy of everyone who plays the mobile game "Arsenia Chronicle" (the "Game").

1. Personal data we collect — none

The Game does not collect any personal data. There is no account or sign-in, no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and nothing is sent to our servers.

2. Data stored only on your device

Save progress, settings (sound, language, difficulty, etc.) and whether the full game is unlocked are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the Game. (If device backup is turned on, the operating system may include the Game's data in your account backup.)

3. In-app purchase

Unlocking the full story (Chapter 6 onward) is handled by App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google) in-app purchase. Payment details are processed by Apple or Google and never reach us. The Game only checks your store purchase history on the device to confirm the unlock. Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's privacy

Because the Game collects no personal data, it does not collect personal data from children.
